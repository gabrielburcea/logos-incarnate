# Product Roadmap

## Direction

Logos Incarnate is a meaning-first Bible study platform centered on reading, manuscript-style annotation, contextual meaning, graph exploration, genealogy, and long-term visual storytelling.

The roadmap should prioritize proving the core product difference before attempting the full platform.

## Phase 1 — Genesis 2 Vertical Slice

Goal: prove the core experience in one chapter.

Includes:
- chapter reader
- Reading Mode / Study Manuscript Mode
- persistent highlights, underlines, and handwritten-style notes
- Meaning Explorer for selected targets
- small graph preview

## Phase 2 — Meaning Explorer Expansion

Goal: extend meaning-first study beyond the initial vertical slice.

Includes:
- more words, people, and places
- stronger occurrence analysis
- deeper contextual summaries
- expanded linked passage support

## Phase 3 — Graph and Genealogy Expansion

Goal: make relationships more visible across the biblical world.

Includes:
- expanded graph explorer
- genealogy graph layer
- person and place exploration
- tribe and nation relationships

## Phase 4 — Multi-Translation and Structured Data Expansion

Goal: broaden the reading and interpretation foundation.

Includes:
- more translations
- stronger original-language linkage
- broader structured biblical datasets
- improved grounding for AI-assisted interpretation

## Phase 5 — Story and Animation Workflows

Goal: bring biblical meaning and chapter movement to life visually.

Includes:
- storyboard workflows
- visual scene planning
- theme-driven story views
- long-term animation-style outputs
