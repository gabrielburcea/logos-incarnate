# Product Journal

## Purpose

This journal keeps a dated record of how the product vision evolves over time.

It is meant to complement the founder notes by preserving milestones in thinking, shifts in direction, new insights, and strategic decisions that should be easy to revisit later.

## Entries

### 2026-05-23 — Product direction expanded beyond ESV-only planning

The product direction moved away from being understood primarily as an ESV-centered Bible study app.

The stronger long-term direction is a multi-translation, meaning-first Bible study platform grounded in original-language understanding, contextual analysis, and guided interpretation.

### 2026-05-23 — Product differentiated around meaning, not information overload

A core principle was clarified:

The app should not compete by showing more raw information than existing Bible study tools.

Instead, it should help readers understand the core meaning of words, people, places, themes, and passages with clarity first, then depth.

### 2026-05-23 — Meaning Explorer identified as a core product feature

A major feature direction was defined around Meaning Explorer.

This feature should help readers tap a word, person, place, or theme and receive:
- core meaning
- occurrence count
- key related passages
- contextual analysis
- interpretive summary

### 2026-05-23 — Knowledge graph clarified as visible but layered

The graph should be visible as part of the product, but it should not overwhelm the user.

The preferred direction is:
- small graph previews by default
- an expandable full graph explorer
- filters for words, people, places, themes, passages, and genealogy

### 2026-05-23 — Genealogy positioned as part of the graph system

Genealogy remains an important feature, but it should be understood as one layer of the broader biblical knowledge graph rather than a disconnected standalone tool.

### 2026-05-23 — Reading experience redefined as a living study manuscript

A major product distinction was clarified:

Users should be able to transform the reading text into a personal study manuscript with highlights, underlines, handwritten-style notes, and visual connectors that remain visibly attached to the reading surface.

### 2026-05-23 — AI direction clarified as grounded and agentic

The product may use an AI-native or agentic architecture, but the AI should not generate uncontrolled noise.

The preferred model is:
- structured biblical data
- graph-supported context
- AI-generated grounded analysis
- controlled and layered presentation

### 2026-05-23 — Animation storytelling added to long-term vision

The product direction expanded to include the ability to bring ideas, passages, and themes to life in an animation-style storytelling format.

The recommended first step is storyboard-style visual flows before any full animation or video pipeline.

### 2026-05-23 — Best implementation starting point identified

The strongest initial implementation direction was clarified as a narrow, polished vertical slice rather than a broad system.

Recommended first build:
- Genesis 2 chapter reader
- Study Manuscript Mode
- persistent notes
- Meaning Card for selected words
- small graph preview

This would provide a meaningful demonstration of the product's distinctiveness.
