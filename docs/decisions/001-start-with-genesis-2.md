# Decision 001: Start with Genesis 2 Vertical Slice

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-05-23

## Context

The product vision is broad and includes:
- multi-translation Bible reading
- meaning exploration
- graph exploration
- genealogy
- immersive manuscript notes
- AI meaning generation
- animation storytelling

Attempting to build the full system first would add too much complexity and delay validation of the product's core value.

A focused starting point is needed that demonstrates what makes the app unique.

## Decision

The project should begin with a narrow, polished vertical slice centered on Genesis 2.

The first implementation should aim to combine:
- chapter reading
- Study Manuscript Mode
- persistent highlights and handwritten-style notes
- Meaning Explorer for selected words
- a small graph preview

## Why This Decision Was Made

Genesis 2 provides a strong demonstration passage because it contains:
- meaningful key words
- people and relationships
- theological depth
- contextual interpretation opportunities
- graph and genealogy potential

This makes it ideal for proving the product direction without requiring the whole Bible or full infrastructure from the beginning.

## Consequences

### Positive
- faster validation of the app's differentiating value
- easier to refine UI/UX before scaling
- lower implementation risk
- better founder and user feedback loop

### Tradeoffs
- the initial vertical slice will not represent the full product scope
- some stakeholders may mistake the chapter-specific vertical slice for the final scope unless explained clearly

## Revisit Conditions

This decision should be revisited once the Genesis 2 vertical slice demonstrates a satisfying experience for:
- reading
- note-taking
- meaning exploration
- graph visibility
